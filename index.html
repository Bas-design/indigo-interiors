The Decorator

Mia Szukalski

Mia discovered her passion for design while building her own home — and never looked back. Leaving a career in Information Technology, she retrained as an interior decorator and founded Indigo Interiors in 2017: a boutique design studio dedicated to creating refined, personal spaces that reflect the people who live in them.

“Guided by style, defined by you.”

Drawing on design influences gathered from her travels and adapted to Australian architecture and lifestyle, Mia works with homeowners and property developers alike — with creativity, clarity, and care, every detail thoughtfully considered.
