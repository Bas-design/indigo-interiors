What We Do

Services

From a single considered colour to a whole-home transformation — each service is shaped around your space, your life, and your budget.

01

Colour & Finishes Selection

The palette is the soul of a home. Mia develops whole-home colour schemes that flow from room to room, pairing paint colours with the finishes that bring them to life.

  • Interior & exterior paint colour schemes
  • Finishes boards: flooring, benchtops, tiles, tapware
  • On-site colour consultations with large-format samples
  • New build & renovation selections packages

02

Materials Specification

Every surface, fabric and fixture — researched, sampled and documented. You receive a complete specification your builder, joiner or trades can work from without guesswork.

  • Fabric, stone, timber & hardware curation
  • Detailed specification schedules for trades
  • Sourcing from trade-only suppliers
  • Budget-conscious alternatives, always

03

Wallpaper & Painting Services

From a single statement wall to a full interior repaint, we specify the product, manage trusted installers and painters, and make sure the finish is flawless.

  • Wallpaper selection & professional installation
  • Interior & feature painting, managed end-to-end
  • Specialty finishes: limewash, murals, panelling
  • Colour matching to existing furnishings

04

Home Organisation

Order is a form of beauty. We design storage and organisation systems for the hardest-working spaces in your home — so everyday life feels effortless.

  • Wardrobe, pantry & laundry systems
  • Decluttering & restyling sessions
  • Custom joinery briefs & storage planning
  • Home office & study setups

05

Furniture & Styling

The finishing layer: furniture, lighting, art, rugs and objects selected and arranged so the whole room reads as one composition — yours.

  • Furniture selection & space planning
  • Soft furnishings: curtains, blinds, cushions, rugs
  • Art & accessories curation
  • Property styling for sale

06

Design Consultations

Not sure where to begin? A one-off consultation gives you a clear direction — practical advice, a considered plan, and the confidence to move forward.

  • In-home or virtual consultations
  • Room-by-room action plans
  • Renovation & new build guidance
  • Mood boards & concept direction

How It Works

The Process

Consultation

We meet in your home, walk the spaces together, and talk about how you live and what you love.

Concept

Mia develops a direction — palettes, materials and mood — presented clearly so you can picture the result.

Selection

Every colour, finish and piece is sampled, refined and documented, ready for trades and suppliers.

Reveal

Installation and styling are managed to the last detail — then the doors open on your finished home.
